GenLayer Asimov

Two sides argue a motion on chain. A round of GenLayer validators names the winner.

Who argued a case better is a judgement no formula can make. Open a motion, let one side argue for and another argue against, each committing their argument in their own transaction. Then anyone can ask a GenLayer round to read both and name the more convincing side. Neither side can force the win, the contract fetches nothing, and "I win" is not a winning argument.
